Unblended

A tool for the moment you are in.

When a part of you has taken the wheel, the steps for getting some room are simple and almost impossible to recall. This app holds them for you so you do not have to.

This is not a replacement for therapy. It is a tool for handling a hard moment and understanding yourself a little better between sessions. It cannot know your history, notice what you are avoiding, or be there when something opens up. A good therapist does all three.

  • No AI. Nothing you write is read or processed by a language model.
  • No server. There is nowhere for it to be sent.
  • No account, no tracking, no cost. Ever.

Your entries are saved in this browser only. That also means clearing your browser data erases them, so export a copy now and then.
