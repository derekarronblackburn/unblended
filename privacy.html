Privacy Policy

Unblended. Last updated 29 July 2026.

The short version: nothing you write leaves your device, because there is nowhere for it to go. Unblended has no server, no account, no analytics, and no third-party code. It is a set of static files that run in your browser.

What is collected

Nothing. No personal information, no contact details, no identifiers, no usage data, no diagnostics, no advertising data, no location, no contacts, no health records. There is no sign-up and no account to create.

What you write, and where it is kept

Your session entries, the parts you name, and your settings are saved using your browser's local storage. That is a store on your own device, readable only by this site in this browser. It is never transmitted anywhere.

Because there is no server, nobody, including the author, can read your entries, recover them, or be compelled to hand them over. There is no copy to hand over.

How the data is used

Only to show it back to you inside the app: your journal, your list of parts, and the summary of your own patterns. It is never analysed for any other purpose.

Artificial intelligence

None is used. Nothing you write is sent to, processed by, or used to train a language model or any other machine learning system. The prompts in the app are fixed text, written in advance, and identical for every person.

Third parties

There are none. No analytics provider, no crash reporting, no advertising network, no fonts, scripts, or assets loaded from anywhere else. The app has no dependencies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may process standard server request information such as IP addresses for delivery and security. That is GitHub's processing, not the app's, and it happens whenever you load any page anywhere. Nothing you type is part of it.

The app contains one outbound link, to findahelpline.com, a directory of crisis lines. Following it takes you to a site with its own policy.

Retention and deletion

  • Retention is entirely yours. Data stays until you remove it.
  • Delete a single entry with the delete button inside that entry.
  • Delete everything by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the app if you added it to your home screen.
  • There is no request to make and nobody to ask, because there is no server-side copy. Deletion on your device is complete deletion.

The other side of that: clearing your browser data erases your entries permanently, and nobody can restore them for you. Use Export in the Journal tab to keep a copy.

Consent

There is no data processing to consent to, and so nothing to revoke. Using the app is the whole of the arrangement.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Not a medical service

Unblended is a self-help practice aid. It is not therapy, not a medical device, not a crisis service, and it does not diagnose, treat, or measure anything. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by the IFS Institute, Richard Schwartz, or any clinical body.

If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number. In the US you can call or text 988. findahelpline.com lists free crisis lines by country.

Changes
